Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock translations in the Bhagavad Gita?

All translations are completely free and unlocked. Simply navigate to any Shloka in the Bhagavad Gita section and you can read the meaning in English, Hindi, or Gujarati.

How do Dharma Points (DP) work?

You earn Dharma Points by completing your daily quests, such as chanting mantras or reading the Bhagavad Gita. Accumulating points will level up your Spiritual Profile and unlock new ranks.

How do I share a Shloka on social media?

When viewing a Shloka, tap the Share icon. You can customize the background image and language, and a beautiful poster will be generated for you to share directly to Instagram, WhatsApp, or other platforms.

Is my data backed up?

Yes, if you sign in using Apple or Google, your streaks, Dharma points, and progress are securely backed up to the cloud.
